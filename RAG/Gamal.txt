### spice-overview
Investigation: SPICE, Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment (PSI-107)
Topic: overview and data limits
Sources: Challenge brief 'Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights'; PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv); computed from the PSI-107 Experimental table by spice_prepare.py; file names seen in the PSI-107 'SPICE Day 045' folder, contents not read yet

SPICE (PSI-107) is the Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment, a microgravity combustion experiment on the
International Space Station that burns gaseous fuel flames in a flow of coflowing air to study when a flame starts to
emit smoke (soot). [Source: Challenge brief 'Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights'; fuels from PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv)]
It was run in the Microgravity Science Glovebox with the fan-driven flow duct that SPICE introduced; the same duct was also
used for BASS, BASS-II and SLICE. [Source: Challenge brief 'Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights']

The experiment table lists the test conditions of each run: GMT day, ignition time, flame-off time, fuel, nozzle diameter,
fuel rate (sccm, mg/s and hydrocarbon "HC" mg/s) and coflow air velocity (cm/s). [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv)]
The original table has 469 rows; after removing 34 exact duplicates, 435 records remain, which group into
147 flames (a flame is one ignition on one GMT day). The runs cover 11 GMT days (day 45 to day 174),
6 fuels and 3 nozzle diameters. [Source: computed from the PSI-107 Experimental table by spice_prepare.py]

IMPORTANT LIMIT: this table contains conditions only. It has no smoke-point result and no flame length. [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv)]
Smoke-point and flame-length values are expected in other PSI-107 files (Spice_Flight_Flamelength.xls and
SmokePointReview-0g-3.xlsx), which have not been read or joined yet. [Source: file names seen in the PSI-107 'SPICE Day 045' folder, contents not read yet]

Data-quality notes: 12 flames have no usable ignition time (entries such as LOS, loss of signal);
12 flames have no coflow air velocity (records with a zero or negative value were set to missing);
18 flames have no burn time because the ignition or flame-off time is missing. [Source: computed from the PSI-107 Experimental table by spice_prepare.py]

### spice-glossary
Investigation: SPICE, Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment (PSI-107)
Topic: terms and columns
Sources: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv); general knowledge, not from the PSI files

GMT day: day of the year (Greenwich Mean Time) on which the run happened. [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv), column 'GMT date']
Flame: one ignition on one GMT day; one flame can have several table records because the fuel rate was changed during the
burn. [Source: definition chosen in spice_prepare.py; assumption]
Nozzle diameter (mm): the burner tube the fuel flows from; the table uses 0.4064, 0.762 and 1.6002 mm. [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv)]
Coflow air velocity (cm/s): speed of the air flowing past the nozzle. [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv), column 'Air Rate (cm/s)']
Fuel rate: sccm = standard cubic centimetres per minute; mg/s = mass flow. [Source: PSI-107 Experimental table (PSI-107_Experimental_table_Spice.csv), column names]
HC fuel rate: the hydrocarbon part of the fuel flow, assumed to differ from the total only for diluted blends. [Source: assumption]
Smoke point: the flame condition (fuel flow or flame length) at which a laminar diffusion flame starts to emit soot. [Source: general knowledge, not from the PSI files]

### slice-overview
Investigation: SLICE, Structure and Liftoff In Combustion Experiment (PSI-106)
Topic: overview and what the airflow files contain
Sources: Challenge brief 'Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights'; PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle'; PSI-106 InfoTxt.doc.docx; computed from PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx by slice_prepare.py

SLICE (PSI-106) is the Structure and Liftoff In Combustion Experiment, a microgravity combustion experiment on the
International Space Station. It was run in the Microgravity Science Glovebox in the same fan-driven flow duct as SPICE. [Source: Challenge brief 'Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights']

The data in this corpus are airflow boundary-condition measurements: the speed of the air near the burner nozzle at different
fan settings, measured with a probe at many positions. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle']
463 measurements are included, from measurements made in 2009 and 2012; the source sheet marks the 2012 set in yellow and says
the two sets agree well. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle'; computed from PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx by slice_prepare.py]
Scans: X direction at 4 mm and 1 mm above the nozzle, Y direction at 4 mm and 1 mm above the nozzle, and a Z (vertical) profile at
(X, Y) = (9, 0) mm. The nozzle centre is the origin (0, 0). [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle'; PSI-106 InfoTxt.doc.docx]

ASSUMPTIONS: velocities are taken to be in cm/s and positions in mm; the source sheet does not state units. [Source: assumption]
These files describe the airflow only. They contain no flame result such as smoke point or flame length. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle']
Near the nozzle the flow is not uniform: velocity is lowest right above the nozzle centre (the nozzle's wake) and higher
off to the sides. [Source: computed from PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx by slice_prepare.py]

### slice-fan-calibration
Investigation: SLICE, Structure and Liftoff In Combustion Experiment (PSI-106)
Topic: converting the AIR display reading to velocity
Sources: PSI-106 SLICE_fan_calibration_20140828.xls; PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle'

The SLICE and SPICE duct fan was set with a fan setting and a fan reading, and the AIR display showed a number that can be
converted to coflow velocity. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_BoundaryCondition.xlsx, sheet 'Variation near Nozzle'; PSI-106 SLICE_fan_calibration_20140828.xls]
The pre-launch calibration polynomial is u = -0.0044 x^2 + 1.2899 x - 11.828, where x is the AIR display value (valid from 17 to 63)
and u is velocity in cm/s. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_fan_calibration_20140828.xls]
The calibration summary notes that the flow degraded over the campaign (soot accumulation), so the conversion is approximate. [Source: PSI-106 SLICE_fan_calibration_20140828.xls]
For example an AIR display of 35 gives about 28.0 cm/s. [Source: computed from the polynomial above]
