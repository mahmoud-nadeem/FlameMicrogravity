SOURCE_URLS: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220005205/downloads/ACME%20CSSCI%20paper%2020220402.pdf?attachment=true

CONTENT:
In a normal spherical diffusion flame, fuel exits the burner into an oxidizing environment. In an inverse arrangement, oxidizer exits the burner into a fuel-containing environment. The configurations change reactant transport and the fuel-rich region; comparisons must include the flame orientation.
