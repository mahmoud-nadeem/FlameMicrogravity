CONTENT:
ACME studies laminar non-premixed gaseous flames under reduced gravity aboard the ISS. Its investigations share combustion hardware but pose distinct questions: soot minimization, flame stability, induced ion transport, spherical flame extinction and emulated material burning. Do not pool data across experiments without harmonizing their experimental designs.

SOURCE_URLS: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220005205/downloads/ACME%20CSSCI%20paper%2020220402.pdf?attachment=true

CONTENT:
Reducing buoyancy-induced flow allows long residence times and approximately spherical flame configurations that may be difficult to sustain under normal gravity. It also changes radiative losses, soot production and extinction behavior. A microgravity result is not automatically transferable to a terrestrial fire scenario.
