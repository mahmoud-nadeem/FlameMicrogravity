SOURCE_IDS: SCI20
SOURCE_URLS: https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/investigations/bre/

CONTENT:
The BRE burner presents gaseous fuel through a flat porous surface. It measures feedback of heat to that surface because a real solid or liquid depends on feedback for vaporization or gasification. Matching selected heat and smoke characteristics is a model of material burning, not a direct test on every solid or liquid.
