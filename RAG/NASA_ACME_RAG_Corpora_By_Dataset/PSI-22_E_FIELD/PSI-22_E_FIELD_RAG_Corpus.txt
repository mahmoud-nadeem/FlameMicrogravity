CONTENT:
Electric potential (V or kV), local electric-field intensity (V/m or kV/cm), applied polarity, mesh distance and ion current (A or µA) are different physical variables. Keep their original units and measurement provenance. Never compute field strength as voltage divided by an assumed gap.
