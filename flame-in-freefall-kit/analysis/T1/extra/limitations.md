T1 limitations

Recorded oxygen is not a limiting oxygen index. No material hazard ranking or causal comparison. Sample labels may be reused. PSI-26 oxygen fraction conversion is supported by original workbook formulas and NASA BASS documentation; airflow is not standardised across investigations. Date corrections marked provisional require original records; no claim of scientific verification. Raw videos were not analysed.

V6: contextual material labels are hypotheses; numeric airflow sequences are display entries, not timing or extinction data. CO negatives and censoring are not exact concentrations; oxygen screening sensitivity is not a physical threshold. RAG retrieval fixtures have not been evaluated against a deployed retriever.

V7 publication: NASA/TM-20210011385 Appendix A (Tables A.1, A.3, A.6) shows missing underlying O2 readings for B2, P22A, P22B, T28A, T28B. Their five reported final O2 readings are excluded from the analytical final O2 and change fields; two other final O2 readings are not reported. There are 122 usable pairs. Negative CO, nonnumeric and overrange readings are not exact concentrations; explicit censoring bounds remain. Raw source values and audit records are retained outside the primary RAG run fields. No fabricated replacements.
